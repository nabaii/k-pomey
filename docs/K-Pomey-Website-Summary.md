**Where the website stands**

The site is built and working. What follows is what it cost, what it costs to

## Built

### A complete six-page website, with an editor you can run yourself.

Home, Work, Flowers, Care, Studio and project pages — plus a visual editor for
changing projects, photographs, prices and contact details without a developer.
Enquiries route straight to WhatsApp as a structured message, so there is no
server to pay for and nothing to break.

It scores **100 out of 100** on Google's mobile checks for speed, accessibility
and search readiness, and loads in about 12 KB — built for a metered Abuja
connection.

## Running costs

### ₦152,000 a year to keep the site online.

Two recurring payments — the hosting each month and the domain each year. Both
should be held in the business's own name, so the accounts stay with you.

| Service | What it is for | Cost | Per year |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Hosting** (Render) | Serving the site, HTTPS padlock included. The paid plan keeps the site awake so it opens instantly for every visitor. | ₦11,000 / month | ₦132,000 |
| **Domain name** | The web address itself, renewed yearly. | ₦20,000 / year | ₦20,000 |
| **Business email** | A working `hello@` inbox on your own domain. | ₦0 (free plan) | ₦0 |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Where every enquiry lands. The normal free app. | ₦0 | ₦0 |
| **Visitor analytics** | How many people visit, and how many tap through to WhatsApp. | ₦0 (free plan) | ₦0 |
| **Total** | **To keep the site online** | **₦11,000 / month** | **₦152,000 / year** |

Hosting and the domain bill in US dollars, converted here at roughly ₦1,550 to
the dollar — the naira figure moves with the rate, so confirm at signup. Email
and analytics have free plans this site fits inside comfortably; a Google
Workspace mailbox, if you want one, is about ₦11,000 per person per month on top.

One free item worth more than any of the above: a **Google Business Profile**.
It puts the firm on Google Maps for "landscaping Abuja", and for a local trade
it usually returns more enquiries than the website itself.
