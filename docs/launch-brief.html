Where the website stands

The site is built and working. What follows is what it cost, what it costs to run, and the few things still needed to put it live.

Built

A complete six-page website, with an editor you can run yourself.

Home, Work, Flowers, Care, Studio and project pages — plus a visual editor for changing projects, photographs, prices and contact details without a developer. Enquiries route straight to WhatsApp as a structured message, so there is no server to pay for and nothing to break.

It scores 100 out of 100 on Google's mobile checks for speed, accessibility and search readiness, and loads in about 12 KB — built for a metered Abuja connection.
