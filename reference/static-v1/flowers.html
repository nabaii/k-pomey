03Two ways to buy

Once, or every season.

Both start the same way — we come and look at the space. What differs is what happens after the flowers are in.

Option A

One-off supply & install

You need colour now — a frontage before an event, a reception refreshed, a bed replanted. We quote the plants and the labour together, deliver and plant in one visit, and leave you a short care note.

Option B

Seasonal rotation

Flowers are a wasting asset in Abuja heat. On a rotation agreement we replant on a set schedule and keep the beds fed and watered between changes, so the frontage never has a dead month.
