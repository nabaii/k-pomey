Poolside garden

Abuja 2025 7 weeks On monthly care

The site as we found it

A raw pool shell in an open compound. Bare laterite to the boundary, no shade, and a fall running back toward the house — which meant every heavy rain put water where nobody wanted it.

The owners wanted somewhere to sit in the afternoon, not just a pool to look at. That turned a surfacing job into a garden: shade first, then planting to hold the ground, then paving set to move water away rather than across.

What we did

  • Levels re-cut and falls set away from the house
  • Sub-surface drainage to the compound boundary
  • Paved pool surround, laid to fall, non-slip finish
  • Royal palms set on a rhythm along the long edge
  • Kerbed planting beds, soiled and mulched
  • Turfing to the remaining open ground
  • Low-level garden lighting to the path and beds
